Neighborhood Watch

An incremental game about watching. It starts with a doorbell camera.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you do here is sent anywhere. The game keeps a save file in this browser's local storage and nothing else. If, late in the game, it asks to use your camera, the video is shown only to you and is never recorded, uploaded, or stored; you can decline, and the game continues either way. "Delete all footage" erases the save.

The company in the game is invented. The incidents are modeled on reporting about real license plate reader networks, retail face recognition, school laptop cameras, workplace monitoring, and the people organizing against them (see deflock.org and flocksurveillance.org). The form is borrowed, gratefully, from Frank Lantz's Universal Paperclips.
