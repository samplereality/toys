About the American Atlas

    
In January 2025 the Gulf of Mexico was renamed the Gulf of America
       by executive order. The American Atlas simply follows
       that logic to its natural conclusion: every body of water in North
       America — American, Canadian, and Mexican alike — is now named
       America, and every national park, monument, and landmark is now
       Trump-something. Navigation may become challenging. That is a
       small price to pay for greatness.

    
This is satire. A parody project. Not affiliated with
       any government, bureau, executive order, or golf course. Except for the
       Gulf of Mexico (renamed in this timeline too), the renamings here are
       fictional — for now.

    
Zoom in to reveal ever-smaller waters that are also named America.
       Click any label for its official renaming decree. Toggle
       “Reveal former names” to commit light cartographic thoughtcrime.

    
Built with Leaflet.
       The entire map — land, borders, lakes, rivers — is drawn from
       Natural Earth data
       (public domain). No tile servers, no API keys: the Bureau trusts no
       foreign cartography. The only labels left untouched are countries and
       states. Click any lake or river; they are all named America now.
       Coordinates approximate; patriotism absolute.
