About The Atlas of America

In January 2025 the Gulf of Mexico was renamed the Gulf of America by executive order. The Atlas of America (formerly the American Atlas — the Board renames its own publications too) simply follows that logic to its natural conclusion: every body of water on Earth is now named America, and every national park, monument, and landmark is now Trump-something. The Board began with North America; under Phase 2: The World, the renaming now extends from the Nile (The American River) to Mount Everest (Mount Trump — the tallest one). Navigation may become challenging. That is a small price to pay for greatness.

The Board assumed the duties of the U.S. Board on Geographic Names (est. 1890), whose name was the Board's first renaming.

This is satire. A parody project. Not affiliated with any government, bureau, executive order, or golf course. Except for the Gulf of Mexico (renamed in this timeline too), the renamings here are fictional — for now.

Zoom in to reveal ever-smaller waters that are also named America. Click any label for its official renaming decree. Toggle “Reveal former names” to commit light cartographic thoughtcrime.

Built with Leaflet. The entire map — land, borders, lakes, rivers — is drawn from Natural Earth data (public domain). No tile servers, no API keys: the Board trusts no foreign cartography. The only labels left untouched are countries and states. Click any lake or river; they are all named America now. Coordinates approximate; patriotism absolute.
