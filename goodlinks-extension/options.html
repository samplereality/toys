Article Content Download

After saving via the API, also fires goodlinks:// to trigger GoodLinks to download the full article for offline reading. Chrome will ask to open GoodLinks the first time — check "Always allow" in that dialog to make it silent. Disable this if you don't need offline content or find the prompt disruptive.

Defaults

Comma-separated tags to pre-fill when saving. You can change them per-save.
